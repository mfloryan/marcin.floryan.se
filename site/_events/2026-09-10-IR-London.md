A full-day [Innovation Roundtable](https://innovationroundtable.com/){:target="_blank" rel="external"} workshop on *Transforming Culture &amp; Work with AI &amp; Agents*, hosted by Google Cloud at Pancras Square, London.

Two contributions from me:

- **Talk — _Evolving Leadership for a New Reality_.** As AI speeds up how work gets done, decisions get made faster and more in isolation: individual output can rise while alignment, collaboration and collective intelligence slip. Drawing on nearly a decade at Spotify and my current role leading software at Marshall Group, I share five pillars of leadership and why they matter more, not less, in a world shaped by AI.
- **Roundtable — _Psychological Safety_.** A facilitated session where leaders assemble a personal toolkit of practical steps for building an innovation culture that treats mistakes as learning.

Other sessions feature speakers from Google Cloud, IKEA, Bosch, Philips and Mars.
